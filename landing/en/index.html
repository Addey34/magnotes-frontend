One board, four ways to see it

Built for freelancers and professionals who want a real work-notes tool — not just another to-do list.

🗂️

Piles that fan out

Drop a card on another and they form a pile. Click it to spread it into a fan of side tabs, and drop one pile onto another to merge them. The canvas stays readable, even with 200 notes.

🔗

Mentions & backlinks

Reference a card with [[mention]]: the link is clickable and the cited card shows who cites it. You can also link cards with typed arrows.

⌨️

Ctrl+K palette

Fuzzy search across all your boards, quick note capture, view switching and template insertion, all from the keyboard.

📋

Canvas, Kanban, Agenda, Timeline

The same cards as a free-form board, as status columns, grouped by due date, or unrolled along a timeline.

✅

Cards that organize

Markdown, checklists, status, priority, due date, tags and images: a card stays a note, but can also track a task.

✋

Move first, edit second

Grab a card anywhere. An Edit button opens text, status, due date and checklist. No more accidental edits.

🔒

Read-only sharing

Generate a link to show a board to a client or colleague: they view it without an account and cannot change anything.

🚀

Try it without an account

Demo mode runs in your browser: no sign-up needed, and it even works offline.

📝

Your notes stay yours

Export to Markdown or full JSON, delete your account and data in one click. No lock-in.

Coming from somewhere else? Import your notes.

Import a file from the app menu: each note becomes a card, ready to sort.

  • Markdown (.md, .txt)
  • Trello (JSON export)
  • Notion (CSV export)

Limits: 1 MB per file and 100 cards per import.
